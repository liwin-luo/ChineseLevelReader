## How to get a portrait that actually looks like your pet

This is the part that separates good results from "whose cat is this?" Input quality beats everything else.

**Start with one clear, front-facing photo.** Face fully visible, eyes open, good light. This is the anchor the AI builds the likeness from.

**Add one or two more angles if you have them.** A side profile and a seated shot help the AI keep the face and fur consistent across the image — that's why the upload allows up to four photos.

**Keep it solo.** One pet per portrait, no humans in the frame. The tools are built for pets only, and group shots or blurry phone photos are the most common reason a generation comes back mediocre. If a result misses, don't pay for a retry — regenerating is free, and a sharper photo usually fixes it.

**Mind the file basics.** JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 5 MB each. A crisp recent photo beats a scanned photo from 2010.
